<h2>✨ Features</h2>
<ul>
  <li>📅 <b>Daily Challenge Tracker</b> – Mark each day’s problem as <i>done</i> or <i>skipped</i>.</li>
  <li>🔥 <b>Streak Counter</b> – Don’t break the chain, build consistency.</li>
  <li>📊 <b>Progress Dashboard</b> – Visualize completion with charts & stats.</li>
  <li>🌗 <b>Dark/Light Mode</b> – Switch vibes anytime.</li>
  <li>💾 <b>Local Storage Persistence</b> – Your progress stays even after refreshing.</li>
  <li>📱 <b>Responsive Design</b> – Smooth experience on desktop & mobile.</li>
  <li>🎯 <b>Gen‑Z Styled UI</b> – Emojis, gradients & clean minimal design.</li>
</ul>
